# Platform Ecosystem, Channels, and SDKs

## Category

Overview of the industry / platforms subtree. Coverage: platform and channel ecosystem (01), mini-game platform constraints (02), the four categories of SDK (03), release versioning and business models (04).

## Definition

This chapter explains why the platform ecosystem can profoundly reshape a project's technical form. Channels, mini-game platforms, login and payment, ads, and social SDKs are not merely integration work; they are part of distribution and of the business model.

The core problems these records touch:

- How platform capabilities constrain product and technical solutions
- How risk isolation is achieved during SDK integration
- The coupling points between platform versioning and business model
