# Engine Selection, Language Map, and Tooling Ecosystem

## Category

Overview of the client / engines subtree. Covers: engine business models (01), the language map (02), project and team selection (03).

## Definition

This set of appendices puts engines, languages, and the tooling ecosystem back into their real commercial and team contexts for discussion, recording the context that needs to be brought into a selection decision. It is not a ranking.

The core questions these notes deal with:

- The relationship between technology stack choices and project conditions
- The long-term costs that come from business models, team structure, and the tooling ecosystem
- The gap between reputation and actual constraints

## Related
