## Definition

In a game project, a single word often carries product semantics, engineering semantics, and organizational semantics at the same time, and it is a common occurrence for different roles and different projects to each use their own wording. This section pulls the book's high-frequency terms back into one shared context to explain them. The point is not to chase an industry-wide single standard, but to make the terms support fast judgment: who owns it, where the state lives, when changes take effect, and who catches failures.
