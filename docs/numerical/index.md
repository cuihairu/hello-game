# Planning and the Numerical System

## Category

Overview of the numerical tree. Node positioning: numerical design is the quantified language of gameplay — unfolding from the design side the conventions (01), the two main trunks (02 combat / 03 growth), the two systems (04 economy / 05 randomness), the two experience-tuning dimensions (06 monetization / 07 difficulty), and the reading list (08); written from a programmer's perspective, the implementation side and Feature Note 02 are two sides of the same coin.

## Definition

Numerical design is the quantified language of gameplay: the sense of growth, combat pacing, economy loop, drop surprises, difficulty curves — all of it is numbers speaking. This chapter unfolds the planning and numerical system from the design perspective — working conventions, combat numbers and damage formula family selection, growth curve models, economy loop, drops and probability, the structure of monetization numbers, level difficulty and new-player pacing, and finally a verifiable reading list and references.

This chapter is the counterpart to Feature Note 02 "Numerical and Economy Systems": the Feature Note records server-side numerical implementation (attribute pipeline, deterministic randomness, pity ceiling state machine, currency ledger), while this chapter records design-side numerical definitions and verification. Both sides share the same terminology and can be cross-checked on the same questions.

> Note: all specific numbers in this chapter concerning monetization pricing and deployment pacing are **example models, not industry standards**; the formulas and methods are public general practices.
